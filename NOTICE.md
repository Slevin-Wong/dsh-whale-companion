# Notice

## Artwork

The sixteen portraits shipped in this plugin are cropped from the public fan pack:

- Repository: https://github.com/the-beating-light-of-the-nail/deepseek-chan-meme-pack
- Gallery: https://aigengtu.com/ (梗鲸 GengJing)
- Used previews: `previews/007`, `010`, `022`, `035`, `037`, `040`, `044`, `050`, `052` (center-cropped), `070`, `071`, `077`, `081`, `083`, `085`, `123`

Selection rule: every square preview in the repository is a candidate; a non-square preview joins only when its longer-to-shorter side ratio is at most 1.2 and a center crop applies. That excludes `056` (1.40), `058` (1.33), `087` (1.40) and `097` (1.33).

If you own rights to any image and want it removed, open a takedown request in the pack repository; the same image will be dropped from this plugin.
